Restoring replays the uploaded dump with psql: all objects in the target database are dropped and replaced (backups contain DROP ... IF EXISTS statements). The uploaded dump is kept as a local backup and appears in Executions.

Stored as {db}-pre-restore-*.sql in local backups and recorded in Executions. The restore is skipped when this dump fails.
When the profile's database is missing on the server, it is created from template0 before restoring; the pre-restore backup is then skipped (nothing to back up).
Drops the target database (force-closing its connections) and recreates it empty from template0, so the dump replays into a clean slate — no already exists errors on re-imports. The pre-restore backup, if enabled, runs before the drop.
Replay the dump as-is: OWNER TO and GRANT/REVOKE statements are kept — every role they mention must exist on the target. Unchecked, they are dropped (--no-owner --no-privileges) and objects are owned by the profile's user.
